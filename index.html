Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know.

What is Cyro? +
Cyro is a digital time capsule app. You write a message, add photos or a prediction, choose an unlock date, and seal it. Nothing can be opened before that date — not by you, not by us. On the day you chose, it unlocks and delivers itself.
Can I send a capsule to someone else? +
Yes. When creating a capsule you can choose "someone else" and add their email. They'll receive a notification that a capsule is waiting, and another when it unlocks on the chosen date.
Can I edit a capsule after sealing it? +
No. Like a time capsule buried underground, once sealed it cannot be changed. That's the point — the version of you that sealed it is preserved exactly as it was.
What happens if Cyro shuts down? +
We'd never just disappear with your capsules. If we ever had to wind down, we commit to warning you in advance and releasing every pending capsule early — your messages delivered, not lost. The most durable copy is the one you download on opening day, and we make sure you get it.
Will my capsule survive 10 or 20 years? +
We've built on durable infrastructure and take preservation seriously. We won't make unbackable permanence claims — but what we can promise is: on opening day you download everything in standard formats and keep your own copy. The best insurance is the one in your hands.
How much does it cost? +
One payment when you seal a capsule. No subscription, no recurring bill, no surprise charges years later. You seal it, you own it.
